Privacy policy

Last updated November 7, 2026.

Who we are

This website is operated by Surrey Fingerprint Company, 112-8232 120 St, Surrey, BC V3W 3N4. Contact: info@surreyfingerprint.com.

What this website collects

You do not need to give us your name or any personal details to browse this site. We do not collect your application information here.

When you select an Online Criminal Record Check button, we send ourselves a notification containing the time, the page you were on, your approximate city and region, whether you used a mobile or desktop device, and the page you came from. It does not include your name, email address or ID details. To avoid duplicate notifications, a short-lived anonymous code derived from your connection may be held for about two minutes and then deleted.

We do not currently use advertising or analytics cookies on this site.

Your application

When you select the button, you move to our screening partner’s website (everify.reliabilityscreening.ca) to complete your application. Your personal information is collected and handled there, under that site’s own privacy terms. Please read them before you apply.

Service providers

Our website is hosted on Cloudflare, and notifications are delivered through an email service provider. These providers process the technical data described above on our behalf.

Your rights and questions

You may ask us what information we hold about you, or ask us to correct or delete it, by emailing info@surreyfingerprint.com. We handle personal information in line with applicable Canadian privacy law, including BC’s Personal Information Protection Act and PIPEDA.

Changes

We will update this page if our practices change, for example if we add website analytics.

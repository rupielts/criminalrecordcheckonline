Name-based vs fingerprint-based criminal record check

Both checks look for a criminal record, but they work differently and are accepted for different purposes. Ask the organization requesting your check which one it needs.

Name-based checks

A name-based check is quick to request online. Because it relies on your name and date of birth rather than fingerprints, some organizations accept it for general screening but not for every role.

Fingerprint-based checks

A fingerprint-based check confirms your identity against your fingerprints, which makes it the more definitive option. Immigration and visa processes commonly require one, as do some agencies and licensing bodies. Surrey Fingerprint offers digital and ink-and-roll fingerprinting at 112-8232 120 St, Surrey, BC.

Working with children or vulnerable people?

Roles in the vulnerable sector can require a more detailed check than a standard criminal record check. Ask the organization exactly what it requires, or call us on 604-825-0177 and we will point you the right way.

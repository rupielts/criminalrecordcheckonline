Quick answers about CPIC, CRJMC, online applications, prices and fingerprinting.

Common questions

What is a CPIC criminal record check?

CPIC stands for Canadian Police Information Centre, the national system police services use to share criminal record information. A CPIC-based check searches it using your name and date of birth.

What is a CRJMC?

CRJMC stands for Criminal Record and Judicial Matters Check, a name-based check that some employers and organizations request by name. Confirm the exact requirement with the organization asking for it.

Can I get a criminal record check online?

Yes. A name-based check can be requested online by completing a secure application and verifying your identity, with no in-person visit.

How much does an online criminal record check cost?

A CPIC check is $45 and a CRJMC check is $50, in Canadian dollars.

What do I need to apply?

A device with an internet connection, your personal details and government-issued ID.

Is a name-based check the same as a fingerprint check?

No. A name-based check uses your name and date of birth. A fingerprint-based check uses your fingerprints and is the more definitive option. Read the comparison.

Which check do I need?

The organization asking for the check decides. Ask which type it accepts: CPIC ($45) or CRJMC ($50).

Can I apply from Ontario, Alberta or Manitoba?

Yes. The online application is open across Canada. Ask the organization requesting your check which result it accepts.

Will my employer accept this check?

That is your employer’s decision. Ask which type of check it accepts before you apply.

CPIC and CRJMC: what they mean

CPIC

The Canadian Police Information Centre is the national database that police services across Canada use to share criminal record information. A CPIC-based check searches it using your name and date of birth.

CRJMC

The Criminal Record and Judicial Matters Check is a name-based check commonly requested for security licensing in British Columbia. Always confirm with your licensing body which check it requires.

Common questions

What is a CPIC criminal record check?

CPIC stands for Canadian Police Information Centre, the national database police services use to share criminal record information. A CPIC-based check searches that database using your name and date of birth.

What is a CRJMC?

CRJMC stands for Criminal Record and Judicial Matters Check, a name-based check that is commonly required when applying for a security licence in British Columbia. Confirm the exact requirement with your licensing body.

Can I get a criminal record check online?

Yes. A name-based check can be requested online by completing a secure application and verifying your identity, with no in-person visit.

Is a name-based check the same as a fingerprint check?

No. A name-based check uses your name and date of birth. A fingerprint-based check uses your fingerprints and is the more definitive option. Some employers and agencies require one specifically.

Criminal record check for a security licence

Applying for a security guard or security worker licence? Most licensing bodies ask for a criminal record check with your application. Here is how to get the right one.

Online Criminal Record CheckClick here

Which check do security licence applicants need?

In British Columbia, a CRJMC (Criminal Record and Judicial Matters Check) is commonly requested for security licensing. In Ontario, Alberta, Manitoba and other provinces, the required check depends on the licensing body and the licence type, so confirm before you pay. A CRJMC is $50 and a CPIC check is $45.

Steps

  1. Check your licensing body’s instructions to see which check it accepts and how recent it must be.
  2. Select Online Criminal Record Check and order that check.
  3. Verify your identity online with government-issued ID.
  4. Submit your result with your licence application as your licensing body directs.

Common questions

Do I need a CRJMC for a BC security licence?

A CRJMC is commonly requested for security licensing in British Columbia. Always confirm with your licensing body before ordering.

Can I apply online for a security licence criminal record check?

Yes. You can apply online for a CPIC or CRJMC check from anywhere in Canada.

How much is a criminal record check for a security licence?

A CRJMC is $50 and a CPIC check is $45, in Canadian dollars.

Does this apply outside BC?

You can apply from any province. Whether the result is accepted is decided by the licensing body in your province.

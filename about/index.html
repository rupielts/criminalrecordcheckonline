About Surrey Fingerprint Company

Surrey Fingerprint Company is a Surrey, BC business accredited by the RCMP for fingerprinting. We help people with criminal record checks, fingerprinting, immigration and visa services, and security guard licensing.

What we do

  • RCMP criminal record checks
  • Digital and ink-and-roll fingerprinting
  • Immigration and visa services
  • Security guard licensing services
  • Online CPIC and CRJMC name-based criminal record checks, through this website

About this website

criminalrecordcheckonline.com makes it easy to request a name-based criminal record check online from anywhere in Canada. When you select the button, you finish your application on our screening partner’s site. This is a private business website, not an official RCMP or Government of Canada website.
